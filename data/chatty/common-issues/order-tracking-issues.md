Q: The Order Tracking feature is not showing in the chatbox.
Q: Customers can't find the order tracking option in the chat widget.
A: The Order Tracking block must be enabled in the chatbox settings.

**Solution:**
1. Go to Chatbox > General > Blocks.
2. Ensure "Order Tracking" is toggled ON.
3. If enabled but still not showing, check Display Rules to ensure the chatbox is visible on the page.
4. Verify the App Embed is turned on in Shopify Admin > Online Store > Themes > Customize > App Embeds.

---

Q: A customer says order tracking shows "No tracking info" even though the order has been shipped.
Q: Order tracking is not displaying shipping details.
A: This can happen when:

1. The merchant hasn't added tracking numbers to the order in Shopify.
2. The tracking provider isn't supported by Shopify's native tracking.
3. The tracking information hasn't synced yet.

**Support flow:**
1. Ask the merchant to verify the order has a tracking number in Shopify Admin > Orders.
2. Check if the tracking provider is supported.
3. If tracking info exists in Shopify but doesn't show in Chatty, escalate to TS team.

---

Q: The merchant wants to customize what information shows in order tracking.
Q: Can I change the order tracking layout in the chatbox?
A: Order tracking displays standard Shopify order data (order number, status, tracking number, delivery status). Currently, the layout and displayed fields are not customizable.

If the merchant needs more customization, log the feedback and escalate to the product team as a feature request.
