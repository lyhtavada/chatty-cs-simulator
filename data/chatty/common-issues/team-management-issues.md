Q: How do I add team members to Chatty?
Q: The merchant wants to invite agents to their Chatty account.
A: Guide the merchant through the team setup:

1. Go to the Chatty app > Team section.
2. Click "Invite member" and enter the team member's email.
3. The invited member will receive an email with setup instructions.
4. They can log in via Shopify Admin (Apps > Chatty) or app.chatty.net.

**Plan limits:**
- Free: 1 member
- Basic: up to 5
- Pro: up to 10
- Plus: Unlimited

If the merchant needs more members than their plan allows, recommend upgrading.

Q: Conversations are not being auto-assigned to agents.
Q: All conversations show as "Unassigned" even with auto-assignment enabled.
A: Check the assignment settings:

1. **Verify assignment mode:** Go to Settings > check if "Automatic Assignment" (round-robin) is enabled.
2. **Check agent availability:** Agents must be online/available to receive auto-assigned conversations.
3. **Check "Reassign conversations" settings:** Ensure the reassignment rules are configured correctly.

If everything looks correct but conversations still aren't assigned, collect conversation details and escalate to TS/dev team.

Q: A team member can't access the Chatty app.
Q: Invited agent gets an error when trying to log in.
A: Common causes:

1. The team member hasn't accepted the invitation email yet.
2. They're trying to log in with a different email than the one invited.
3. They need to access Chatty through Shopify Admin first to activate.
4. The merchant's plan limit has been reached.

**Solution:** Verify the invitation status, confirm the correct email, and guide them to open Chatty from Shopify Admin first.

---

Q: The merchant wants to remove a team member from Chatty.
Q: How do I delete an agent from the team?
A: To remove a team member:

1. Go to Team section in the Chatty app.
2. Find the team member and click remove/delete.
3. Their active conversations will become "Unassigned" — the merchant should reassign them first.
4. The removed member will no longer have access to the inbox.
