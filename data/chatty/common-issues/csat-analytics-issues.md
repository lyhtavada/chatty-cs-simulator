Q: How do I enable the CSAT survey for a merchant?
Q: The merchant wants to collect customer satisfaction ratings.
A: CSAT (Customer Satisfaction) survey is available on Pro and Plus plans only.

**Setup:**
1. Verify the merchant is on Pro or Plus plan.
2. Go to Settings > CSAT section.
3. Enable the CSAT survey.
4. Customize the survey message if needed.
5. CSAT survey will automatically appear after conversations are resolved.

If the merchant is on Free or Basic, recommend upgrading to Pro for CSAT access.

Q: The merchant wants to see analytics for their FAQ page.
Q: Where can I find FAQ performance data?
A: FAQ analytics are available in the Chatty app:

1. Go to FAQ Builder > Analytics.
2. View metrics: page views, question clicks, search queries.
3. Use search query data to identify gaps — questions customers search for but can't find answers to.
4. Recommend the merchant add new FAQ entries based on popular searches.

Q: The merchant wants to know how many AI conversations they've used this month.
Q: How do I check AI conversation usage?
A: To check AI conversation usage:

1. Go to **Subscription** in the left sidebar. Under **Usage this cycle**, the AI conversation card shows current usage vs. quota (e.g. 2 / 50).
2. Plan limits: Free (50/month, resets monthly), Basic (100/month), Pro (500/month), Plus (1,000/month).
3. If approaching the limit, recommend upgrading or purchasing extra conversations at $0.40 each (not available on Free — the AI simply pauses until the next monthly reset).
